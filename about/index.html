About B-Atlas

B-Atlas is an independent boat knowledge and research resource built to help people move from a broad idea of the boat they want to a smaller set of models worth serious investigation.

What B-Atlas is trying to solve

Boat research is fragmented. Specifications may be spread across manufacturer brochures, owner groups, archived manuals, broker listings and forum discussions. Model names change, production runs evolve, and older boats are often documented inconsistently. B-Atlas brings that information into a common structure so models can be researched and compared more easily.

Community knowledge

Specifications alone rarely tell the whole story. B-Atlas is designed to improve through corrections, manuals, photographs, model history and practical ownership experience. Contributions are reviewed before they become part of the permanent knowledge base.

Help build B-Atlas

Independent and free to use

B-Atlas is intended to remain useful first: a research resource rather than a marketplace search engine. The site is free to use. Voluntary support helps cover the cost of maintaining and expanding the project.

Support B-Atlas

Research carefully

B-Atlas describes boat models, not the condition of an individual vessel. Production changes, optional equipment, refits and owner modifications can make two examples of the same model materially different. Important dimensions, machinery and condition should be verified on the specific boat before purchase.
