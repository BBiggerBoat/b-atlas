Plan your boating

Understand what you want from a boat

Work through the questions before searching. Firm requirements remove known poor fits; preferences improve ranking; unknown information remains visible.

Step 1 of 6

Where do you expect to cruise?

Broad cruising areas help you think about weather, range, shelter and local boating style. They do not remove models. Select the closest match, or choose Other to continue without using this choice as a filter.

Specific waterways with hard limits Optional

Select these only when you expect the model to use that waterway. Bridge clearance, locks, draft or range may exclude known poor fits.

What is your preferred length and beam?

Use maximums only when a larger model would create a real ownership problem. Confirm local marina slip limits, haul-out capacity and winter-storage restrictions. Unpermitted road trailering is commonly limited to about 8 ft 6 in wide; wider boats generally require permits and may need specialized transport.

Which fuel and propulsion arrangements suit you?

Fuel type affects efficiency, purchase cost and service needs. Propulsion affects simplicity, shallow-water access and close-quarters manoeuvring. Select only arrangements you would reject alternatives for.

Engine arrangement

A single engine is simpler and usually less expensive to maintain. Twins add propulsion redundancy and can make close-quarters manoeuvring easier, but add a second engine, controls and running gear to service. Select an arrangement only when engine count is a firm requirement.

Which features matter most to you?

Standard Preferences do not normally eliminate a model. They help B-Atlas rank known matches higher. Unknown features remain possible, with lower confidence.

Who will use the boat?

Crew Fit helps rank layouts that are more practical for the people aboard. It does not reject a model when the relevant information is missing.

B-Atlas Boat Watch

Create Boat Watch

B-Atlas prepares monitoring instructions from your selected models. You can use them with ChatGPT to create an ongoing Boat Watch, or copy the same instructions into another AI.

Using Boat Watch with ChatGPT
  1. Review the search area, price limit and any additional requirements below.
  2. Click Copy & Open ChatGPT. B-Atlas copies the complete monitoring request and opens ChatGPT in a new tab.
  3. Paste the copied instructions into ChatGPT and send them.
  4. Ask ChatGPT to run the search as a recurring monitoring task and confirm how often it should check. When recurring tasks are available on your ChatGPT account, it can notify you when a genuinely new qualifying listing appears.

You can return to B-Atlas at any time and create a new watch with different models or requirements.

Move / Share Saved Models

Take your Saved Models to another device or browser without an account, or share a shortlist with someone else.

What do you want to do?

Selected modelsThe models checked on the Saved Models page will be included.

How to move it: Copy the link, then open that link in the other device or browser. B-Atlas will show the incoming models before anything is changed. Choose Merge — Keep Newer or Replace Saved Models.

The transfer is encoded after the # symbol in the link. That fragment is handled by the browser rather than sent as part of the normal page request to B-Atlas. No account is required and B-Atlas does not store the transfer on its server. Treat the link like any other copied personal note: anyone you give it to can read the information you chose to include.

Saved Listings are not transferred. Only Saved Model records are affected.
