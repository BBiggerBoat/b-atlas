Silverton 34 Convertible Generation 1 Convertible

1978–1988

The first Silverton 34 Convertible generation spans the original 1978–82 boat and its 1983–88 update. B-Atlas keeps them in one generation because the later version is documented as using the same solid-fiberglass hull; year-scoped production phases preserve the changes in fuel capacity, bridge clearance and interior arrangement.

Trade-offs

  • Twin gasoline inboards increase fuel and maintenance cost
  • Flybridge height and windage affect route and docking considerations
  • Condition and refit history matter substantially on older examples

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect deck, flybridge and window penetrations for moisture
  • Inspect fuel tanks, exhaust, cooling, wiring and steering
  • Verify engines, transmissions, shafts, struts and rudders
  • Confirm model year against generation-specific dimensions and tankage
