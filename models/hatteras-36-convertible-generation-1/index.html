Hatteras 36 Convertible Generation 1 Convertible

1969–1977

The Hatteras 36 Convertible Generation 1 is a distinct generation of the 36 Convertible. It is kept separate from the other generation because the production runs are separated by a gap and the hull dimensions, displacement and tankage differ materially.
