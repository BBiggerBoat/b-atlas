CHB 34 Double Cabin

1972–1985

The CHB 34 Double Cabin is a compact Taiwan-built flybridge cruiser with a long prop-protecting keel, walkaround decks and separate forward and aft sleeping cabins. Most examples use a single diesel shaft drive and cruise at displacement speeds. Construction details changed materially during production, especially the cabin house, decks, flybridge and window framing.

Best for

  • Couples wanting private guest accommodation in a 34-foot trawler
  • Inland and coastal cruising at displacement speeds
  • Buyers comfortable evaluating and maintaining an older Taiwan-built cruiser

Trade-offs

  • Early plywood cabin houses and teak-fastened decks demand careful moisture inspection
  • Extensive exterior teak can create substantial maintenance
  • Single-screw handling requires technique or docking assistance
  • Builder and year differences make individual verification essential

Inspection focus

  • Moisture-test teak-deck fasteners, deck penetrations, window surrounds and cabin-house joints
  • Inspect fuel tanks, fill and vent hoses, supports and access for corrosion or leakage
  • Inspect original wiring, shore-power equipment, bonding and later electrical modifications
  • Inspect exhaust systems, engine mounts, shafting, rudders and steering gear
  • Confirm builder, model configuration and major refit history from hull documentation
