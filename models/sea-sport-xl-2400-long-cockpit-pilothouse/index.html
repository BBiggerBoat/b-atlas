Sea Sport XL 2400 Long-Cockpit Pilothouse

From 1999

The Sea Sport XL 2400 is a 24-foot Pacific Northwest hardtop cruiser/fishing boat with an 8-foot-6-inch beam, deep-V hull, large working cockpit and compact four-berth cabin. Current production uses outboard power while preserving the enclosed all-weather character associated with the long-running Sea Sport line.
