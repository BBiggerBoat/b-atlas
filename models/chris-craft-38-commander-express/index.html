Chris-Craft 38 Commander Express Express Cruiser

1964–1972

The Chris-Craft 38 Commander Express is a distinct factory layout built on the original 38-foot Commander fiberglass hull. B-Atlas keeps it separate from the sibling 38 Commander layouts because cockpit, bridge and interior arrangements differ materially.

Trade-offs

  • Large twin-inboard systems increase operating cost
  • Original tanks, wiring, decks and machinery are now several decades old
  • Layout-specific weight and clearance differ across the 38 Commander family

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect stringers, engine beds and structural tabbing
  • Inspect deck, cabin and window penetrations for moisture
  • Inspect fuel tanks, exhausts, wiring and steering
  • Verify engine, hardtop and flybridge configuration
