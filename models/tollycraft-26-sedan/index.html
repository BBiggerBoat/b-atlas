Production updates

  • 1977: anodized window frames replaced earlier extruded aluminum frames.
  • 1982: interior decor changed from wood-grain mica to teak.

Inspection focus

  • Confirm exact engine, V-drive and fuel-system configuration.
  • Inspect window frames and surrounding structure for leakage.
  • Inspect deck, flybridge and cockpit penetrations for moisture.
  • Inspect shaft, rudder, steering and underwater gear.
  • Verify tank condition and actual capacities.

About this record

B-Atlas preserves uncertainty. The gasoline V-drive is documented as the most common configuration, so fuel type and engine count remain configuration-dependent rather than being used as universal exclusions.
