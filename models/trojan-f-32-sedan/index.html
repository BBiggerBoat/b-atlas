Trojan F-32 Sedan Flybridge Sedan

1973–1991

The Trojan F-32 Sedan is a long-running fiberglass flybridge sedan produced from 1973 through 1991. Its 13-foot beam, bright wraparound-window cabin and straightforward twin-inboard layout made it one of Trojan's most successful models.

Trade-offs

  • Wide beam produces substantial interior space but limits some slips and transport options
  • Twin gasoline inboards are common and can be fuel intensive
  • Early and late boats differ in deck materials and fuel capacity
